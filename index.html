Services

Engineering support from initial design through factory acceptance and startup, for new equipment and existing lines.

Controls programming and commissioning

PLC logic and HMI development for packaging and material handling equipment, taken through startup on the floor.

  • Machine sequencing, fault handling, and reset logic
  • Servo motion and variable frequency drive integration
  • Vision and sensor integration for inspection and reject

Control panel and electrical design

Schematics and panel design that are buildable, serviceable, and fully documented.

  • Electrical schematics and redlines
  • Drive, motor, and component sizing
  • Bills of materials and engineering change control

Troubleshooting and field support

Root-cause diagnosis for equipment that is down, intermittent, or never ran right.

  • Industrial network and drive communication faults
  • Sensor, pneumatic, and I/O issues
  • Retrofits and control upgrades on existing machines

Power systems engineering

Facility power distribution and protection, informed by nuclear-grade design practice.

  • Switchgear and motor control center design
  • ETAP modeling and protective device coordination
  • Class 1E safety-related electrical design
