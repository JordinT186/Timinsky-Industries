Scope

What's included

Network and communication faults

Modbus RTU and RS-485 diagnosis: wiring, termination, serial settings, and addressing.

Drive and motion faults

Variable frequency drive and servo faults traced to parameters, wiring, mechanics, or logic.

Sensors and I/O

Misfiring, double-triggering, and wiring-polarity problems, with replacement sensor selection when the original is wrong for the job.

Pneumatics

Valve and solenoid faults, including electrical causes like supply voltage sagging under load.

Retrofits and upgrades

Adding functions to existing machines, often by repurposing I/O already in the panel.

Commissioning support

Extra hands and a second set of eyes when a startup is running long.
