Scope

What's included

PLC programming

Machine sequencing, interlocks, fault handling, and reset logic in ladder, built to be read and maintained by the next technician.

HMI development

Operator screens for run, setup, alarms, and diagnostics, designed around how the line is actually operated.

Servo motion

Commissioning and tuning of servo axes for indexing, orienting, and positioning applications.

Drive integration

Variable frequency drive selection, parameterization, and control over Modbus RTU and hardwired I/O.

Vision and sensing

Barcode verification, fiber-optic and photoelectric detection, and PLC-timed reject mechanisms.

Commissioning and startup

On-site bring-up, I/O checkout, sequence debugging, and handoff to production.
